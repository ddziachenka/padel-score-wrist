# M16 App Review notes draft

Independent watchOS app with a real iPhone companion. No login or developer account.

iPhone: Start provides installation and usage help. Demo contains Match and Americano; both use the real scoring engines without saving games or consuming free games. Match starts 2–2, 40–30 under Golden point and ends after one game; Americano starts 10–10 of 24 total rallies with two serves per player. Undo and Reset remain available after each demo ends. Access offers the existing non-consumable com.dziachenka.padelscore.lifetime and Restore purchases. Purchase is allowed before Watch installation; real games require Apple Watch.

Watch runs without the iPhone app installed or connected. All original scoring, correction, result/history and launcher journeys remain on Watch. Five completed games are free; About → Access permits purchase before the limit. Purchase and restore need internet. Each device verifies Apple transactions independently using the same Apple Account; no transferred Boolean is trusted. Open Access/Restore on the other device while online to receive the purchase.

Launch offer: app download is free. Lifetime IAP is 0 in all available regions at launch; users must confirm the App Store transaction to retain lifetime access forever. The offer covers public launch day plus seven full days; afterward the prior regional prices return (US base 4.99). The final date is scheduled after actual publication. AFTER_APPROVAL remains intended.

This is preparation copy only. The new binary must pass M16 and a separate release-readiness revalidation before submission.
