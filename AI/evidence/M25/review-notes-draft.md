Independent Apple Watch app (watchOS10+) with an iPhone companion (iOS17+).
Real games are scored on Watch. iPhone provides interactive learning/demos and
optional lifetime purchase/restore; its gesture button explicitly simulates input.
No sign-in or demo account is needed. Watch gameplay works without the phone.

Gesture scoring is optional and OFF for every new game. On a supported Watch with
watchOS11+ and Double Tap enabled in system settings, enable Gesture scoring in
Match/Americano setup or the current game's menu. Keep the playable score visible
and screen awake. One Double Tap series means TWO thumb/index contacts and proposes
US. A second series before the original three-second deadline selects THEM; a third
cancels. At the deadline exactly one rally is saved. Touch team buttons cancel the
proposal and score immediately; Undo restores the complete preceding score/service.
Leaving the score or lowering the wrist cancels pending input. Try practice from
setup/menu; it never changes a real game or consumes free games.

For selective correction: score at least three rallies, then Menu → Journal → an
earlier rally. Change the winner, remove a duplicate or insert a missing rally.
Review Before/After score, serving and finish status. Later recorded winners stay
in order. Resolve serving explicitly where a changed set boundary requires it.
Apply once; one Undo restores the complete original record. Cancel or a save failure
preserves the original. A manual Edit score replaces the current situation and
creates a checkpoint; it does not rewrite unknown earlier rallies. Archived detailed
journals are read-only; legacy history truthfully says detail is unavailable.

For a mid-game start: select Match or Americano, choose Starting score → Current
score, choose rules, enter a continuable score and explicit serving, review and
confirm. Earlier rallies remain unknown and Undo stops at that starting checkpoint.
Match has no fixed set limit or invented overall winner. Optional Players names
can be entered/changed on Watch; archived rosters remain unchanged.

On iPhone Start, try gestures (explicit simulation), historical correction, or a
known-score start. All examples are disposable and independent of real Watch data,
purchases and free-game usage. Demo has ordinary Match/Americano score examples.

The first five completed real games are free. To reproduce the gate, play five
separate short Americano rounds to their total, finishing each. Starting a sixth
new game opens Access; the current game, latest result, History, practice and demos
remain available. The lifetime non-consumable is
com.dziachenka.padelscore.lifetime. Both Watch About → Access and iPhone Access
provide purchase/restore; use the same Apple Account. No subscription. Deleting
local games does not reset usage or revoke a verified purchase. Actual localized
price comes from StoreKit. No temporary promotional price is promised here.

About contains offline Privacy, Terms and Support. Phone and purchase views link
to corresponding public pages. Only purchase/restore and external links need a
network. Names, games and journals remain locally on Watch, without an account,
cloud sync, analytics, HealthKit or location access.
