# M25 active Store materials — work in progress

This set replaces the previous active Watch, iPhone and creative galleries when
its complete capture manifest and validation are finished. Older M15/M16 files
remain historical/source evidence; do not upload them as the recovery gallery.
Nothing here is published or sent to App Store Connect by M25.

Native galleries: gesture scoring, per-game opt-in, agreed-score replacement,
historical rally correction, unrestricted Match sets, Americano, known-score
starts and local History. Phone images show actual guide/simulation/recovery demos,
ordinary Match/Americano demos and optional StoreKit-verified Access. Real games
run on Watch; the phone cannot recognize Watch Double Tap.

Artwork: three built-in imagegen directions, selection and refinement are in
artwork/. One pose illustrates contact; localized text explicitly states that
one series has two finger contacts. Artwork never reconstructs a device screen.

Metadata: fourteen local drafts in metadata/, retaining the final neutral
promotional text, title, subtitle and keywords. New descriptions explain gestures
and recovery without claiming exclusivity. Hardware-dependent language remains
PENDING_M29 and cannot support distribution until M29 passes. Publication and
submission are outside this milestone.
