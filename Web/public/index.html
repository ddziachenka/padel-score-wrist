Gesture scoring

Optional. Off for every new game. One series means touching your thumb and index finger together twice. One series: us. Two: them. Three: cancel. Wait five seconds to save. For THEM, wait for “Pending: US”, then repeat the series: two finger contacts. Requires watchOS 11 and an Apple Watch that supports Double Tap, with the screen awake and system gestures enabled.

Tap your team after each rally. Made a mistake? Undo brings back the score and server.

Try score recovery

Recover an accurate score. Replace the current score with the score everyone agrees on, or change, remove or insert an earlier recorded rally while keeping later winners in order. Review the score and serving consequences before applying; one Undo restores the whole correction. Start Match or Americano from a known score with explicit serving. Earlier rallies remain unknown. Match sets are unrestricted; finish when you choose. Add optional player names on your Watch. Names and games stay local. Selective corrections apply only to the active game or latest editable result, after its last checkpoint.

Match · Americano

Choose Match with sets, tie-breaks, and your deuce rule, or score your own Americano round with a shared rally total and serves per player. Scoring works offline, without an account or an iPhone companion app.

Close the app and return to your saved game. Add the Padel Score launcher to a compatible watch face or Smart Stack for quick access. The launcher opens the app; it does not show a live score.

Read the set scores or final round points. Play again from your latest result to reuse its rules and serving order, then check the first server before starting.

History is available to everyone, even after the free-game limit. It stays locally on this Watch. The app has no transfer between Watches, cloud sync, or cloud backup service. Removing the app or changing Watches may lose your results.
