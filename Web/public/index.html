Padel Score — Wrist · Apple Watch

Keep the score. Stay in the game.

Match and Americano scoring on your Apple Watch. Tap a team to record a rally, see who serves next, and undo a mistaken tap.

Coming to the App Store. The first five completed games are free. Then unlock unlimited games with one lifetime purchase. US price: $4.99; local prices are shown by the App Store. No recurring payments.

Made for the court

  • Match scoring with sets, tie-breaks, and a choice of deuce rules.
  • Americano rounds with a shared rally total and configurable serves per player.
  • Serving order, score corrections, Undo, and a saved game you can return to.
  • Your current game and last result stay available even after the five free games.

On your wrist, offline

For Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Scoring works offline, without an account or an iPhone companion app. Purchases and restoration need access to the App Store. English interface.
