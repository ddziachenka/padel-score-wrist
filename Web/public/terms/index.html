1. License

Padel Score — Wrist uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement. This page explains the app’s access model and support; it does not replace that license.

2. Five free games and lifetime access

The first five completed games are free automatically. You do not need to activate anything or provide payment details to start. After those five games, starting another game requires a one-time lifetime unlock. An existing game, the last result, all finished-game history, and the scoreboard demo remain accessible without purchasing.

The lifetime unlock enables unlimited Match and Americano games in this app. It is a non-consumable purchase with no recurring payments. The US price is $4.99; the App Store shows the applicable local price before you confirm. Payment is handled by Apple. A pending or unverified purchase does not unlock access.

Use About → Access → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Both purchase and restoration need an internet connection to the App Store. Refunds are handled by Apple through Report a Problem; a refunded or revoked purchase may remove unlimited access. Deleting local game data does not reset the free-game count, cancel a purchase, or issue a refund.

3. Scoring and saved data

The app records scores you enter. Check the score and serving order during play; you can undo or correct entries. It is a scoring aid, not a tournament management or official officiating service. Games and finished-game history are stored locally on this Watch. There is no transfer between Watches, cloud sync, or cloud backup service in the app; removing the app or changing Watches may lose them. Settings → Delete local data removes games, finished-game history, Undo history, and saved settings after confirmation while keeping the completed-game count and purchase entitlement.

Try the scoreboard is an isolated, fixed Match demo ending after one game. Its points, Undo, and Reset demo do not change real games or use free games. Play again reuses the latest result’s rules and serving order in a new setup; check the first server before starting. Starting a real game remains subject to the free-game limit.

Read the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details. Help is available on the Support page. No account is required.
