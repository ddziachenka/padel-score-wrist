Padel Score — Wrist · Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 23 October 2026

Padel Score — Wrist is developed by Dzmitry Dziachenka. This policy covers the app, this website, and support email.

1. The short version

The app stores your game on your Apple Watch. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or developer-operated backend. Game data is not sent to the developer.

2. Information processed on your Watch

The app keeps the current game, latest result, all finished-game history with completion dates, scoring and serving settings, Undo history, and a count of completed free games in its private local storage. Apple StoreKit supplies verified purchase information so the app can determine whether lifetime access is unlocked. There is no transfer between Watches, cloud game sync, or cloud backup service in the app. The fixed scoreboard demo stays only in memory while its screen is open. It does not write game data, change history or settings, or use the free-game count.

3. Apple services

Apple handles prices, purchases, payment, restoration, and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Apple may process system diagnostics according to your device settings. The app contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting service and does not request health, location, camera, microphone, or contacts access.

4. Retention and deletion

Settings → Delete local data removes the current game, all finished-game history and results, Undo history, and saved game settings after confirmation. It preserves the completed-free-game count and does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase. Finished games remain in local history until you delete local data; they are not automatically removed or limited by a purchase. Only the latest result can be edited or resumed.

Deleting or reinstalling the app, or changing Watches, may lose local game data and the count. There is no developer backup or recovery service. Device backups follow your Apple settings. Apple retains the purchase record needed for restoration. Local deletion is not a promise of forensic erasure.

5. This website and support email

This is a static Cloudflare Pages website with no forms, analytics scripts, or advertising. Cloudflare processes request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site under its privacy policy.

If you email dzmitry.dziachenka@gmail.com, Dzmitry Dziachenka receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. They are used to answer your request, not for marketing. Messages and attachments are deleted within 90 days after the request is closed, unless a legal obligation or active dispute requires longer retention. Please send only information needed to explain the problem.

6. Changes to this policy

This page and its date will be updated when the practices described here change.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or a request to delete support correspondence, email dzmitry.dziachenka@gmail.com. For help using the app, visit Support.
