Support

Need help with Padel Score — Wrist? Email Dzmitry Dziachenka at dzmitry.dziachenka@gmail.com.

Support is answered by the developer as time allows; no response time is guaranteed.

1. Scoring and saved games

  • A mistaken tap: use Undo on the score screen. For an older mistake, open the game menu and use the correction controls.
  • A new set is waiting: confirm the serving order before recording the next rally.
  • The app closed: reopen it to return to the saved game. A save-error message means the change was not safely saved; free space if needed and try again.
  • A round has reached its total: use Done to keep its result. To resume scoring, increase its rally total when prompted.

Demo, Play again, and history

New game → Try the scoreboard (also in About) opens a fixed Match example. The Demo label keeps it separate from real play. Try a point, Undo, or Reset demo. It ends after one game and does not change saved games, history, settings, purchases, or the free-game count.

On your latest result, Play again opens setup using that game’s rules and serving order. Check who serves first, then tap Start game. History is available from New game, the result, and the game menu. It lists all finished real games, newest first, with detailed results. Older history entries are read-only; only the latest result can be edited or resumed. History remains free after the five-game limit.

About → Watch face & Smart Stack explains how to add the launcher to a compatible watch face or Smart Stack. It opens the app without displaying a live score.

2. Purchases

The first five completed games are free. After that, one lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited games. Your current game, last result, all local history, and demo stay available. There is no recurring payment.

If the price cannot load, check the Watch’s internet connection and tap Retry in Access. If you already paid, open About → Access → Restore purchases using the same Apple Account. Wait for a pending purchase to be approved. Do not send your password or payment details to support.

For a refund, use Apple’s Report a Problem. The developer cannot issue App Store refunds.

3. Delete local data

Open Settings → Delete local data and confirm. This removes the current game, all finished-game history and results, Undo history, and game settings. The completed-free-game count and App Store purchase are preserved. Uninstalling the app or changing Watches may lose local data; there is no transfer between Watches, cloud sync, cloud backup, or developer recovery service in the app.

4. What to include in an email

  • Your Apple Watch model and watchOS version.
  • The app version and build shown in About.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message and, if useful, a screenshot.

Send only what is needed to explain the problem. Never send passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details.

5. Privacy

Support messages are used only to answer your request and are deleted within 90 days after closure, unless a legal obligation or active dispute requires longer retention. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
